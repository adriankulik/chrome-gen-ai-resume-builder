Local AI Resume

A client-side ATS resume tailor and builder designed for maximum privacy and zero cloud dependencies. Your personal career vault, resume text, and target job descriptions are processed and stored entirely on your computer.

🛡️

100% Client-Side Privacy

Zero remote telemetry, no external database, and no cloud servers. All career bullets, contact details, and job descriptions remain stored locally in your browser or Chrome profile.

🧠

On-Device AI Engine

Powered by Chrome Built-in AI (Gemini Nano Prompt API) with zero configuration, or connect to your local model runners (Ollama, LM Studio). Always falls back to instant heuristic matching if offline.

⚡

Dual-Target Companion

Operates as a Chrome Extension side panel directly alongside job postings on LinkedIn, Indeed, and company career portals, or opens in full tab mode for an expansive 3-column workspace.
